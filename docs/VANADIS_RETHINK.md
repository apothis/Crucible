# Vanadis: rethink (draft for the user, 2026-11-08)

## Why the current cut is flat

Measured on the current script (39 scenes, 4 places):

- 18 scenes are a singer facing the lens and singing.
- 13 are empty places where "only the mist / smoke / clouds drift slowly; everything else stays still".
- 8 are a silent singer standing in place and "slowly" turning or lifting their head.
- 0 other people. No Aesir, no giant, no wolf, no war.
- Freyja and Odin never share a frame (the writer is told two-shots are untested and not to write them).
- Nothing the lyric describes happens on screen: no hostage crossing, no teaching, no hanging on the tree, no wall, no wolf.

Every one of those habits comes from a writer rule made after a measured failure (people walking toward the lens, extras moving in unison, two moving elements turning into a timelapse). The rules are right about what breaks, but together they make the writer avoid everything dramatic.

## The spine

Freyja accuses Odin. The verses are the case she makes against him, shown as what happened. The choruses are the verdict: she has foreseen his death and will not save him. The video ends at Ragnarok with his empty cloak in the ash and her watching from her own hall of the dead.

Escalation of the world: grey dawn (the crossing), gold (the hall that caged her), storm (the tree, the wall), fire (the war, the wolf), ash (the end).

## Look

From "heavy, deliberate, tripod-weighted, desaturated" to a look that escalates: cold and grey at the river, warm gold in the hall, storm-lit on the ridge, fire-red and high-contrast at Ragnarok, ash-grey at the end. Bigger camera moves on the plates in the choruses and the solo.

## Writer changes (general, so the next dramatic song gets this too)

1. Places: about one per five scenes (done, commit 3bd6f5c).
2. Show the story: on a narrative lyric, B-roll shows the events the lyric names, with the people in them; empty-place plates at most about one scene in five.
3. Silent shots do something with intent (drives the staff into the ground, throws the cloak back, the tear falls), still one motion per take.
4. Others: gods, warriors, smiths, mourners are allowed wherever the story has them (the existing rules for extras still hold: no unison, small actions).
5. The look follows the story's arc instead of one fixed grade.
6. Two-shots of the two singers: only after the test below passes.

## The rewrite (2026-11-08)

The writer changes (commit 47f4c29) were checked on a second song first. She Never Leaves the Frame, rewritten with no concept given: empty places went from 40% to 15% of scenes, scenes with story characters from 5 to 11, and places from 7 to 9. Every place rule was met.

Vanadis was then rewritten with this draft as its concept (`vanadis_20261108_143602`), and the two pre-chorus cuts were moved so he finishes "pay for what you took" at 87.88 s and 143.96 s. The result:
- 39 scenes in 11 places: 6 new (the hook behind the wall, the falcon cliff, the seeress chamber, the hall of the chosen, the grey shore, the smithy) and 5 kept.
- Empty places down to 10% of scenes; 12 scenes with story characters; 3 silent shots of both singers.
- No writer flags and no pop cuts.

Pictures needed: 6 new places and 8 scenes without singers that must start on their own picture (14 required). Opening pictures are recommended for the 29 singer scenes as well, because the place picture otherwise sets the camera angle.
