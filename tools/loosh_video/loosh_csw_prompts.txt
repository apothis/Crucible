CSW-01  Kale at the gas station (Verse 1)
Loosh inside a small-town gas station at the hot-food counter, hat and sunglasses on, staring in disbelief at the warmer where the fried chicken used to be: it is now full of neat plastic tubs of kale salad with little wooden forks. A hand-lettered sign on the warmer reads exactly FRESH KALE. The clerk behind the counter shrugs. Fluorescent light warmed by the sepia grade, a rack of beef jerky still on the wall behind him.

CSW-02  They put quinoa on the menu (Verse 1)
Loosh at the diner counter holding a laminated menu at arm's length like it is a legal document, reading it through his sunglasses, mouth open, a waitress in an apron waiting with her pad and no sympathy. Behind him the pie case is full of fruit cups. A plate of biscuits on the counter beside him that he has brought himself.

CSW-03  Last man standing (Pre-Chorus)
Loosh standing alone in the middle of the honky tonk dance floor at night, a biscuit held up in one fist like a torch, a beer in the other, chin up, while the entire crowd around him sits at tables eating from salad bowls with their heads down. String lights, the band's gear empty on the stage. Loosh lit, everyone else in shadow.

CSW-04  Can't survive in a salad world (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
Loosh on the porch steps of his shack at golden hour, boots planted wide, one hand on his belly, the other holding a cast-iron skillet of biscuits and gravy out toward the camera like an offering, hat and sunglasses on, defiant and proud. The hound at his boots. A pickup, a smoker trailing smoke, hay bales. Keep the right third of the frame quieter.

CSW-05  Gravy drop (every Drop section)
Night, the field behind the shack. Loosh behind a DJ deck set on a chest freezer, one fist up, two giant subwoofers either side with a banjo cabled in, a smoker on one side billowing smoke, a deep fryer bubbling on the other, string lights, a crowd of cowboys and ravers holding biscuits and glow sticks in the air. Loud, greasy, happy.

CSW-06  The salad bar at the honky tonk (Verse 2)
Inside the honky tonk, a brand-new sneeze-guarded salad bar has been installed where the pool table used to be, all lettuce and cherry tomatoes under cold light. Loosh stands in front of it with his arms folded, hat and sunglasses on, looking at it the way you look at a stranger in your house. The bartender behind him wiping a glass, avoiding eye contact.

CSW-07  Smoothie (Verse 2)
Loosh at a juice bar counter being handed a tall green smoothie by a cheerful young man in a beanie, Loosh holding it at arm's length by the very top of the cup with two fingers, as if it might be alive, his face a mask of polite horror behind the sunglasses. A chalkboard menu behind with no words readable. Bright, clean, wrong.

CSW-08  Pass the gravy (Pre-Chorus 2)
Loosh at a long church-supper table full of people eating salads, standing up at his place, holding a gravy boat high above his head in both hands like a trophy, hat and sunglasses on, roaring. The rest of the table looking up at him, some starting to smile. Casserole dishes, folding chairs, a flag on the wall.

CSW-09  I ain't going nowhere (Bridge, spoken)
Loosh sitting on the tailgate of his truck at dusk in a field, hat pushed back, sunglasses on, slowly eating a biscuit with total calm while looking straight at the camera, a smoker beside the truck trailing a thin line of smoke, the hound asleep in the truck bed. Fireflies. Nothing else happening. Pure stubborn peace.

CSW-10  The whole town comes back (Final Chorus)
Loosh on the porch of his shack at night holding the skillet of biscuits and gravy, and the whole town walking up the dirt road toward him out of the dark, cowboys, the waitress, the gas station clerk, the beanie kid from the juice bar, all carrying their salad bowls upside down and empty, the crowd lit by the porch light and string lights. Loosh grinning. The hound barking.

CSW-11  Gang vocal (Outro)
A huge cookout in the field at night, long tables, the whole town eating biscuits and gravy and fried chicken, Loosh standing on a table in the middle with a biscuit held high, the crowd all holding biscuits up to him, the subwoofers and DJ deck in the background, the salad bar from the honky tonk dumped on its side at the edge of the field with a chicken standing on it. Smoke, string lights, joy.

CSW-12  Ride out (End)
Dawn, the field after the cookout. Tables cleared, smoke still drifting from the smoker, the salad bar on its side in the grass with the chicken asleep on it, and Loosh asleep in a lawn chair with a skillet on his lap and the hound asleep underneath, hat over his face. Warm first light, mist, a single biscuit on the arm of the chair.
