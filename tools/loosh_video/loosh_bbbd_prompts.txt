BBBD-01  Woke up on the porch (Verse 1)
Early morning. Loosh asleep sitting upright in a rocking chair on the sagging front porch of a wooden shack, hat over his eyes, sunglasses still on, mouth open, one buttered biscuit held loosely in his hand on his belly. Empty cans on the porch boards, a hound dog asleep at his boots. Golden low sun through the trees, dew on the rail, sepia print look.

BBBD-02  Truck in the pond, dog in the hat (Verse 1)
A lifted pickup truck nose-down in a muddy farm pond, water up to the headlights. On the bank a basset hound sits wearing Loosh's cream cowboy hat, too big for it. Loosh stands beside the dog in his plaid and overalls, hatless, hair wild, holding a beer, looking at the truck with mild interest rather than alarm. Morning light, mist on the pond.

BBBD-03  Sunday is when it sets in (Pre-Chorus)
Interior of a small wooden country church, Sunday morning. Loosh alone in a back pew, hat on, sunglasses on, slumped and pale, holding a hymn book upside down, a paper cup of coffee on the pew beside him. Sunlight through a plain window, dust in the air, an old lady two pews ahead looking back over her shoulder at him. Deadpan suffering.

BBBD-04  The holy trinity (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
A kitchen table set up like a shrine: a cold can of beer, a buttered biscuit on a chipped plate, and a lit Roman candle firework standing in a coffee mug, with three candles around them. Loosh sits behind the table with his hands folded in prayer, hat on, sunglasses on, eyes closed, completely sincere. Warm candle light, wood panelling, the deer print on the wall behind him. Keep the right third of the frame quieter.

BBBD-05  The drop (every Drop section)
Night, a field party. Loosh stands on the flatbed of a pickup truck behind a DJ deck, one fist in the air, sunglasses on, with two huge subwoofers on the tailgate and a banjo plugged into one of them. A bonfire to one side, a crowd of cowboys and ravers with glow sticks in front, dust and sparks in the air, string lights on the truck. Hay and beer cans everywhere. Sepia print look with the fire glow.

BBBD-06  Planted a keg in the garden (Verse 2)
A vegetable garden behind the shack. Loosh in his overalls waters a beer keg that is half buried in a row of tomato plants, using a tin watering can, tenderly. His mother in a housecoat and curlers watches from the back porch with her arms folded, shaking her head. Afternoon light, chickens in the yard.

BBBD-07  Sixty percent gravy (Verse 2)
A small-town doctor's office. Loosh sits on the paper-covered exam table, plaid shirt open, hat and sunglasses on, holding a biscuit, while a tired older doctor in a white coat holds up a clipboard showing a hand-drawn pie chart with one huge slice. A jar of tongue depressors, an eye chart on the wall, fluorescent light warmed by the sepia grade. Loosh looks pleased.

BBBD-08  Saturday (Pre-Chorus 2)
Inside a honky tonk on a Saturday night. Loosh rides a mechanical bull, one hand gripping, the other holding a can of beer perfectly level, hat on, sunglasses on, expression completely calm as the bull bucks. Crowd around the padded ring cheering, neon signs, a jukebox. Motion in the crowd, Loosh sharp.

BBBD-09  Gang vocal (Final Chorus)
A packed honky tonk bar. Loosh in the middle of a crowd of cowboys, farmers and one confused raver, all with beer cans raised toward the camera in a toast, all shouting the chorus, Loosh loudest, mouth open, can high. Neon beer signs, string lights, sweat and haze. Sepia print look.

BBBD-10  A bad decision coming up right about now (Bridge, spoken)
Dusk. Loosh sits on the open tailgate of a pickup in a field, a biscuit sticking out of the chest pocket of his plaid shirt, a can of beer in one hand and a lit Roman candle in the other, fuse sparking, looking straight into the camera with total calm. The hound dog is walking away out of frame. Last light on the horizon, fireflies.

BBBD-11  Guitar solo (Solo)
Loosh stands on the porch steps of the shack at night playing a battered telecaster guitar, hat tilted back, sunglasses on, cable running to a wall of old amplifiers stacked on the porch, a beer balanced on top of the amps. String lights, moths in the light, the hound dog howling beside him. Hero low angle.

BBBD-12  Ride out (Ending)
Sunrise, the same porch as BBBD-01, from further back. Loosh asleep again in the rocking chair, hat over his face, the hound dog in his lap wearing nothing this time, a neat pyramid of empty cans on the porch rail, a plate with one biscuit left. The truck is back in the yard, still muddy. Warm long light, birds, peace. No text.
