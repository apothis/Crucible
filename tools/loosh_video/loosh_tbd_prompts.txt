THREE BEERS DEEP - shot list for the same ChatGPT conversation
One message per shot. Same character, standing half-strength sepia print look,
landscape 3:2, no text unless stated. Open with:
"Next song: Three Beers Deep. Story: three beers is where Loosh is at his best,
sharp, steady, the man everyone loves. Then he keeps going. The song is him
remembering three beers deep from further and further away. The camera gets
drunk, not him: level at three, tilting a little more with every beer after,
level again the next day. Played completely straight."
=====================================================================

TBD-01  Beer number three (Verse 1)
Loosh at the bar of a quiet honky tonk in the late afternoon, sitting very upright on a stool, three cans of beer in front of him in a neat row, two empty and upside down, the third just opened, both hands flat on the bar either side of it. He looks sharp, steady, content, chin up. Clean, perfectly level composition. Bartender wiping a glass, dust in the window light, jukebox dark.

TBD-02  Sharp as a tack (Verse 1)
Loosh at the honky tonk dartboard, mid-throw, perfectly balanced, three darts already in the bullseye, a half-finished third can on the ledge beside him. A couple of regulars watching with respect. Hat and sunglasses on. Perfectly level.

TBD-03  Keep 'em coming (Pre-Chorus)
Loosh at the bar with the three empties in a row and the bartender setting down the fourth, Loosh giving a small confident nod, the band on the little stage behind just picking up their instruments. The jukebox lit now. A tiny lean to the camera, barely there.

TBD-04  I could walk a straight line (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
Loosh on the honky tonk dance floor walking heel to toe along a strip of tape on the floorboards, arms out like a tightrope walker, chin up, utterly confident, a can in one hand. The bartender and two regulars applauding. Everything perfectly level and sharp, string lights, sawdust. This is the memory of three beers deep. Keep the right third quieter.

TBD-05  The woozy drop (every Drop section)
Loosh behind a DJ deck on the honky tonk stage, one hand on the deck for balance, the other holding a can up, sunglasses halfway down his nose, a wide happy wobble in his stance, two big subwoofers either side with a banjo cabled in, and the entire image tilted and slightly doubled, as if seen through too many beers: lights streaking, a soft ghost image offset from the main one, the crowd a blur of hats and glow sticks. Loud, warm, sideways.

TBD-06  A fella named Clyde (Verse 2)
Loosh at the bar shaking hands warmly with a complete stranger, a skinny old man in a trucker cap, both of them laughing like old friends, four and five empties now in the row, the sixth open. Loosh has one arm round the man's shoulders. The image tilted about eight degrees.

TBD-07  Somewhere in there I lost my chair (Verse 2)
Loosh sitting on the honky tonk floor beside a table, legs out, exactly where a chair should be, looking around for it with mild confusion, a can still upright in his hand, the hound dog sitting on the chair at the next table looking at him. Seven or eight empties on the table above him. Tilted about twelve degrees.

TBD-08  I ain't near done (Pre-Chorus 2)
Loosh back at the bar, a long row of empties now, maybe nine, holding up a hand with all five fingers spread as if that is a number, the bartender pouring anyway, a regular on the next stool with his head in his hands. Tilted, neon streaking a little.

TBD-09  Talking to the jukebox (Bridge, spoken)
Loosh at the jukebox, forehead resting against the glass, one hand flat on it, a can in the other, mouth slightly open mid-sentence, as if deep in conversation with it. His reflection in the glass is offset from him. Hat pushed back. The room behind tilted hard, lights doubled, the crowd gone soft. Warm and sad and funny.

TBD-10  Looking for the line (Final Chorus)
Loosh on his hands and knees on the dance floor at night, searching the floorboards with a flashlight, the strip of tape from the chorus shot running directly under his hand, the whole crowd standing around him in a ring pointing at it, the hound dog sitting on the tape. The image tilted the most of the whole set, nearly twenty degrees, lights streaking.

TBD-11  Hat over face (Outro)
Loosh asleep sitting upright on a bar stool with his back against the bar, hat down over his face, arms folded, a very long row of empty cans beside him, the bartender quietly sweeping. Lights half off. The image is level again.

TBD-12  Tomorrow, about noon (End)
The next day, noon, bright sunlight through the honky tonk windows. Loosh at the same bar stool as TBD-01, upright, fresh, hat and sunglasses on, three cans in a neat row in front of him, the first one just opened, the bartender setting down the other two with a look that says here we go again. Perfectly level, dust in the light.
