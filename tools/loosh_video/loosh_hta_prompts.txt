HONKY TONK HEART ATTACK - shot list for the same ChatGPT conversation

landscape 3:2, no text unless stated. Open with:
"Next song: Honky Tonk Heart Attack. Story: Loosh has a heart attack in a honky
tonk on two-for-one wing night and refuses to leave because the band is playing
his song. Played completely straight."

HTA-01  Two-for-one Tuesday (Verse 1)
Inside a honky tonk at lunchtime, daylight through the blinds. Loosh alone at a table with a mountain of chicken wing bones on a tray, a pitcher of beer half gone, wet wipes, hat and sunglasses on, mid-bite, completely content. A sign on the wall reads 2 FOR 1 WINGS TUESDAY. Neon beer sign off, jukebox in the corner.

HTA-02  The thump (Verse 1)
Same honky tonk, evening now. Loosh standing by the jukebox with one hand pressed flat on his chest over the plaid, the other still holding a beer, eyes wide behind the sunglasses, a small frown, as if listening to something inside himself. The jukebox glows on his face. Couples two-stepping out of focus behind him.

HTA-03  Ain't leaving tonight (Pre-Chorus)
Loosh at the edge of the dance floor, one boot forward, one hand on his chest, the other raised toward the stage in a "one more song" gesture, sweat on his forehead, determined. A three-piece band under string lights in the background, a fiddle player mid-bow. Beer cans on every table.

HTA-04  Somebody call my mama (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
Loosh on his back on the honky tonk dance floor, hat still on, sunglasses still on, one arm flung out, the other holding a beer can upright and unspilled, mouth open mid-holler. A ring of cowboy boots around him as the crowd looks down. Overhead light, sawdust on the floor. Keep the right third of the frame quieter.

HTA-05  Paddles (every Drop section)
A paramedic in uniform kneels over Loosh on the dance floor pressing defibrillator paddles to his bare chest, and the shock has lifted Loosh clear off the floor in a full-body arch, hat flying, boots in the air, beer can still in his fist. Sparks and hay dust in the air, the crowd around them throwing their arms up as if it is the drop, glow sticks and cowboy hats. Big, loud, frozen at the peak.

HTA-06  Doug (Verse 2)
A tired paramedic named on his chest badge as DOUG crouches beside Loosh, who is sitting up on the dance floor, refusing the oxygen mask with one hand and pointing at the stage with the other. An ambulance stretcher waits behind them with a beer already sitting on it. Doug's expression is patience running out.

HTA-07  Boot-scoot boogie on the line (Verse 2)
Loosh line-dancing in the middle of a row of dancers, EKG electrode pads still stuck to his bare chest with wires trailing to a heart monitor on wheels being pushed along beside him by Doug. Everyone else in the line is doing the same step. String lights, sawdust, motion blur on the boots, Loosh sharp.

HTA-08  The doctor's verdict (Bridge, spoken)
A hospital room. Loosh in the bed in a gown that does not close over his belly, hat on, sunglasses on, a beer on the tray table next to the jello. A doctor holds up a long paper EKG strip and the trace on it is clearly the shape of a two-step dance pattern, all peaks and skips. Loosh looks proud. Heart monitor beside the bed.

HTA-09  Flatline (Build-Up before the final chorus)
Close on a hospital heart monitor screen, the green trace gone flat with one long tone, and reflected faintly in the dark screen is Loosh's face behind it, sunglasses on, grinning. The room lights are off except the monitor glow. Very still.

HTA-10  Bury me with the band (Final Chorus, belted)
Loosh back on the honky tonk stage in the hospital gown over his jeans, electrode pads still on, hat and sunglasses on, gripping the mic with both hands and belting, the band behind him going full tilt, the crowd losing their minds, Doug in the front row with his arms up. Sweat, haze, string lights.

HTA-11  Clear! (Outro)
The paramedic paddles held up in the air by Loosh himself, one in each hand, on the dance floor, sunglasses on, hat on, hollering at the crowd like a preacher, the crowd copying him with beer cans held up in each hand. Wide, from slightly above.

HTA-12  Ride out (End)
Dawn outside the honky tonk. Loosh asleep on the ambulance stretcher in the parking lot, hat over his face, gown open, a beer on his chest, Doug asleep sitting on the ambulance bumper beside him, the honky tonk sign switched off behind them. Warm first light, one crow on the sign.
