MRC-01  Flag on everything (Verse 1)
Loosh standing proudly at the end of his gravel driveway at golden hour beside a rusted mailbox with a small American flag taped to it, his muddy lifted pickup behind him with a bigger flag on a pole in the bed, and the basset hound at his boots wearing a tiny flag bandana and looking unimpressed. Loosh has his hand over his heart, hat and sunglasses on, chin up, solemn.

MRC-02  Saluting the microwave (Verse 1)
Inside the cramped 1970s kitchen from the earlier songs. Loosh stands at rigid attention in front of a beige countertop microwave, saluting it, hat and sunglasses on, plaid open, eyes closed with emotion. The microwave door is lit and a plate of biscuits is inside. A small flag magnet on the fridge. Early morning light through the ruffled curtain.

MRC-03  Turn that bass around (Pre-Chorus)
Loosh on the porch steps of the shack at dusk, one hand flat over his heart, the other hand cranking a huge volume knob on a battered amplifier the size of a fridge sitting on the porch, a banjo cabled into it. A small flag flying from the porch post. The hound dog covering its ears with its paws. Deadpan devotion.

MRC-04  Fireworks on a Tuesday (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
Night, Loosh's back yard. Loosh stands on a picnic table with his arms spread wide, hat and sunglasses on, plaid open, beer in one hand, as a huge burst of red, white and blue fireworks explodes in the sky behind him. A hand-painted sign nailed to the fence reads exactly TUESDAY. Smoke, sparks, string lights, the truck and the hound in the yard. Keep the right third of the frame quieter.

MRC-05  Home of the drop (every Drop section)
A county fairground at night. Loosh on the roof of his pickup behind a DJ deck, one fist in the air, a flag cape tied around his neck over the plaid, hat and sunglasses on, two giant subwoofers on the truck bed with a banjo cabled in, fireworks exploding overhead, a ferris wheel lit up behind, a crowd of cowboys and ravers with sparklers and glow sticks. Dust, sparks, confetti. Big and loud.

MRC-06  Mostly a chicken (Verse 2)
Loosh sitting shirtless and proud on a tattoo parlour chair, hat and sunglasses on, showing the camera a large fresh tattoo on his upper arm that is clearly a chicken, drawn like an eagle but unmistakably a chicken, with a small flag behind it. A tired tattoo artist in the background wiping his hands and avoiding eye contact. Flash sheets on the wall. Loosh is delighted.

MRC-07  I cry at the anthem (Verse 2)
Loosh at a county fair at dusk, standing in a crowd with his hat held over his heart, sunglasses on, a single tear running down each cheek, a corn dog in his other hand, in front of a big-box store entrance where a display of plush bald eagles is stacked by the door. People around him are just shopping. Warm evening light.

MRC-08  Hand on your heart (Pre-Chorus 2)
A small-town main street at dusk. Loosh stands in the middle of the road with his hand on his heart and the other arm pointing to the sky, hat and sunglasses on, while a crowd of cowboys, farmers and two ravers behind him all copy the pose. Flags on every lamp post, a pickup with subwoofers parked at the curb, the hound sitting at attention. Low sun, long shadows.

MRC-09  Every one of us is standing (Bridge, spoken)
A honky tonk crowd all standing on their chairs and tables with their hands on their hearts, looking up, lit from above by falling fireworks sparks coming through an open barn roof. Loosh in the middle, standing on the bar, hat over his heart, sunglasses on, chin up, a tear on his cheek, a beer in the other hand. Sparks, haze, flags hanging from the rafters.

MRC-10  Belted final chorus (Final Chorus)
Loosh on a flatbed-trailer stage at the fairground at night, gripping the mic with both hands and belting, hat tilted back, sunglasses on, the flag cape flying, a band behind him with banjo and pedal steel, fireworks finale filling the sky above, the crowd of cowboys and ravers with sparklers and flags losing their minds. Sweat, smoke, confetti.

MRC-11  'Merica! Y'all! (Outro)
From above: a crowd in a dark fairground field holding sparklers and glow sticks, arranged so the glowing points loosely spell out the shape of a star, with Loosh standing in the middle of the star on a hay bale, arms up, flag cape, fireworks smoke drifting. Night, string lights at the edges.

MRC-12  Ride out (End)
Dawn after the party in Loosh's back yard. Spent firework tubes, confetti and cans everywhere, the picnic table, the hand-painted TUESDAY sign on the fence, and Loosh asleep in a lawn chair wrapped in the flag cape like a blanket, hat over his face, the hound asleep across his boots, a small flag still in his hand. Mist, warm first light, one crow on the fence.
