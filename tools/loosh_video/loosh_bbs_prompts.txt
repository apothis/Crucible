BACKROADS & BACKSTRAPS - shot list for the same ChatGPT conversation
One message per shot. Same character, standing half-strength sepia print look,
landscape 3:2, no text unless stated. Open with:
"Next song: Backroads and Backstraps. Story: Loosh loves everything about deer
hunting, the 4 a.m. drive, the thermos, the tree stand, the camo, and has never
once shot a deer, because he falls asleep, or eats, or the deer and his hound
have become friends. The backstraps at the end come out of Dwayne's freezer.
He is not sad about it. Played completely straight."
=====================================================================

BBS-01  Four in the morning (Verse 1)
Pre-dawn, pitch dark. Loosh's muddy pickup on a gravel backroad, headlights on, Loosh behind the wheel in full camo with his cream cowboy hat and sunglasses on in the dark, a thermos in one hand, the hound in the passenger seat wearing a tiny orange vest. Fog in the headlight beams, trees either side, one deer standing calmly on the road ahead that he has not noticed. Sepia print look.

BBS-02  The gear (Verse 1)
Loosh standing proudly beside the tailgate at first light with everything laid out on it like a museum display: a rifle, a bow, three thermoses, a cooler, a camp chair, a bag of biscuits, a bottle of deer scent, a folding table. Hat and sunglasses on, camo jacket over the plaid, overalls. Hound sitting on the cooler. Mist in the trees.

BBS-03  The climb (Pre-Chorus)
Loosh halfway up the ladder of a tree stand, stuck, the ladder bowing, biscuit bag in his teeth, rifle slung, one boot searching for the next rung, hat tilted back, sunglasses on. The hound at the bottom looking up. Golden early light through the trees, a deer watching from the brush behind, unbothered.

BBS-04  Backroads and backstraps (Chorus)  THUMBNAIL
Loosh sitting in a tree stand at sunrise, rifle across his lap, thermos cup in one hand, arms wide to the whole valley like he owns it, grinning, hat and sunglasses on, camo over the plaid. Mist in the valley below, a dirt road winding through it, a herd of deer grazing in plain sight directly under the stand. Keep the right third of the frame quieter.

BBS-05  The snore (every Drop section)
Loosh fast asleep in the tree stand, head back, mouth wide open, hat over his eyes, rifle slid down beside him, thermos tipped and dripping. From his mouth a visible shockwave of sound bends the branches and sends every bird in the forest exploding into the sky, leaves blowing off the trees, and a dozen deer bolting away in every direction below. The hound at the base of the tree with its paws over its ears. Big, loud, funny.

BBS-06  Buck of a lifetime (Verse 2)
A huge twelve-point buck standing ten feet from Loosh in a clearing, calm, looking right at him. Loosh frozen mid-bite with a biscuit halfway to his mouth, gravy on his moustache, both hands full (biscuit and thermos), the rifle leaning against a tree well out of reach, his eyes wide behind the sunglasses. Morning light, mist.

BBS-07  Best friends (Verse 2)
The hound dog and a young deer lying side by side in the grass in a sunny clearing, nose to nose, both asleep. Loosh in the background sitting against a tree with his hat over his face, also asleep, rifle beside him with a bird perched on the barrel. Dappled light, peaceful.

BBS-08  Dwayne's freezer (Pre-Chorus 2)
Inside a cluttered garage, Loosh with his head and shoulders inside a chest freezer, pulling out a white paper package labelled in marker exactly BACKSTRAP, a skinny old man in a trucker cap (Dwayne, the same man from the Three Beers Deep handshake) leaning in the doorway with his arms folded, shaking his head and smiling. A mounted buck on the garage wall. Warm bulb light.

BBS-09  Ain't about the deer (Bridge, spoken)
Loosh sitting on the open tailgate at dusk at the edge of the woods, hat pushed back, sunglasses on, holding the thermos in both hands, looking out at the trees with total contentment, the hound asleep against his leg, the rifle still in its case behind him never opened. Fireflies, last light on the ridge. Nothing happening. Pure peace.

BBS-10  Backstraps on the fire (Final Chorus)
Night, a campfire in a clearing, Loosh crouched over a cast-iron skillet of sizzling backstrap on the coals, flipping it with a fork, hat and sunglasses on, grinning, the hound beside him drooling, camo jacket off and hung on a branch. Beyond the firelight, at the edge of the dark, a ring of deer standing and watching with mild interest. Sparks rising.

BBS-11  Pass it round (Outro)
Around the campfire at night, Loosh and Dwayne and four other hunters in camo and orange, all with plates of backstrap and biscuits, cans raised in a toast, the hound in the middle with its own plate, the pickup with its tailgate down behind them. Every one of them has a rifle leaning against a tree, unused. String lights hung from the truck. Warm, happy, loud.

BBS-12  Ride out (End)
Dawn, the campsite. Fire down to embers, plates stacked, Loosh asleep in the camp chair with his hat over his face and the thermos on his belly, the hound asleep on his boots, and a deer standing right beside the chair eating a biscuit off the arm of it. Mist, warm first light, the rifle still in its case.
